Zemen: Ethiopian Calendar · ዘመን Privacy Policy

Your Time. Your Tradition. Your Calendar.

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Package: com.zemen.calendar

Zemen: Ethiopian Calendar (“the App”) is an Ethiopian & Gregorian calendar application. This policy explains what data we process and why.

1. Summary

  • Calendar math, holidays, fasting, and your personal events stay on your device.
  • Optional ads (Google AdMob) may use advertising identifiers after consent where required.
  • Optional football scores use public third-party APIs when you open that tab.

2. Data we process

  • On-device: preferences, events/reminders, widget payloads.
  • Ads (optional): device advertising ID, coarse location/IP as provided by the ad SDK, crash/diagnostic signals from Google Mobile Ads — subject to your consent choices (UMP).
  • Network: football fixture requests when you use Football; no account is required.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications — event and holiday reminders you enable.
  • Internet — ads, consent forms, and optional live football data.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the in-app Settings → Privacy section or your Play Store listing contact email.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
