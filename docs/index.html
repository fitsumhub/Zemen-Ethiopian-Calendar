Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 November 2026 Package: com.zemen.calendar Controller: FitsumHub

This Privacy Policy describes how Zemen: Ethiopian Calendar (“Zemen”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android application published under package com.zemen.calendar. It is written to support Google Play Data safety disclosures and applicable privacy expectations for a primarily offline productivity application.

1. Who we are

Zemen is developed and published by Fitsum Enunu / FitsumHub (“Developer”). For privacy inquiries related to the App, contact us using the details in Section 10.

The App provides dual Ethiopian and Gregorian calendar tools, Bahire Hasab computus, holidays and fasting information, local events and reminders, home-screen widgets, and optional football scores.

2. Information we process

Zemen is designed so that personal calendar content remains on your device. We do not operate a Zemen cloud account system that stores your events, notes, or preferences on our servers.

Category Examples Storage Shared externally?
Personal events & reminders Event titles, dates, notes, alarm settings On-device SQLite database No
App preferences Language, theme, calendar format, numeral style, feature toggles On-device SharedPreferences No
Home widget display data Current date, weekday, daily saint label On-device widget IPC / local cache No
Optional football data Public match fixtures, scores, standings Transient in-memory / temporary cache Yes — requested from public sports data providers when the Football feature is used
Optional advertising data Advertising ID, coarse device/network signals used by ad SDKs Processed by Google Mobile Ads / consent SDK when ads are enabled Yes — only when advertising is active and, where required, after consent

Calendar mathematics (Ethiopian/Gregorian conversion, Bahire Hasab, saints, and fasting logic) are computed locally. Using the core calendar does not require an internet connection.

3. How we use information

We process information only to operate and improve the App’s advertised features:

  • Display dual-calendar dates, holidays, fasting periods, and related cultural/religious observances.
  • Store and surface personal events, reminders, and home-screen widgets that you configure.
  • Remember your language, theme, and display preferences.
  • Fetch optional public football information when you enable and open that feature.
  • Serve optional advertisements and measure basic ad performance when advertising is enabled.
  • Present and honor privacy/consent choices required by applicable law (for example via Google UMP).

We do not sell personal event content, and we do not use your local event titles or notes for advertising profiles.

4. Android permissions

The App requests only permissions needed for functionality you can see and control:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to deliver event and holiday reminders you enable (Android 13+).
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to fire reminders at the scheduled time where the platform allows exact alarms.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore scheduled reminders after device reboot.
  • VIBRATE / WAKE_LOCK — to support reliable reminder delivery and notification UX.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — solely for optional football updates, optional ads, and consent messaging when those features are active.

You may deny notification permission; core calendar conversion and browsing still work. Reminder delivery will be limited or unavailable without permission.

5. Advertising & consent

The App includes Google Mobile Ads SDK integration for optional monetization. Release builds remain ad-free until production AdMob unit IDs are configured. When advertising is enabled:

  • Ads may be shown on non-calendar surfaces of the App.
  • In regions covered by GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or similar frameworks, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may request consent before personalized advertising.
  • You may manage platform-level advertising preferences through your device / Google settings, and review in-app consent options where available.

Advertising partners may process limited device and usage signals under their own policies. See Google’s advertising privacy materials for details on Google Mobile Ads.

6. Third-party services

Depending on which optional features you use, the App may communicate with:

  • Public sports data providers (for example TheSportsDB and similar public endpoints) to display football fixtures and scores when the Football tab is enabled.
  • Google Mobile Ads / UMP when advertising is enabled, for ad delivery and consent management.

These services process data under their respective privacy policies. Core calendar use does not require those network calls.

7. Data retention & security

On-device event and preference data remain on your device until you delete them in the App, clear App storage, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling Zemen removes local App data stored in the Android application sandbox.

Local data is protected by Android’s application sandbox and the security controls of your device (for example screen lock and OS-level encryption where enabled). No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure; please keep your device protected.

8. Children’s privacy

Zemen is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a channel we control, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Your choices & rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing. Because most personal content never leaves your device, the most direct controls are:

  • Edit or delete events inside the App.
  • Disable reminders and notification permission in Android Settings.
  • Hide the Football tab in App Settings (optional feature).
  • Clear App storage or uninstall the App to remove local data.
  • Manage advertising / consent preferences where ads are enabled.

For privacy requests that require Developer assistance, email us using the contact details below. We will respond within a reasonable period.

10. Policy updates & contact

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The “Effective” date at the top of this page will be revised when material updates occur. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes notice of the revised policy where permitted by law.
