## Full description
Zemen (ዘመን) is your Ethiopian calendar companion — dual Ethiopian and Gregorian dates, Bahire Hasab, Orthodox holidays and fasting periods, personal events, home-screen widgets, and optional live football scores.

**Your Time. Your Tradition. Your Calendar.**

Features:
• Dual Ethiopian ↔ Gregorian calendar with accurate conversion
• Holidays, saints’ days, and fasting guidance
• Personal events and reminders
• Home screen widgets
• Optional football fixtures and tables
• Amharic, English, Oromo, Tigrinya, and French
